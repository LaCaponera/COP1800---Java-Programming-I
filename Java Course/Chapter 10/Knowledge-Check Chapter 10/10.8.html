How this check works
  1. Choose an answer and say how sure you are. Rating your confidence makes it easier to spot what you only think you know.
  2. If your first answer is wrong you get feedback on that choice and a hint, then one more try.
  3. After that you see the reasoning for every option and where it is covered in the reading.
  4. Only first tries count toward your result. Nothing is graded or sent anywhere; progress stays in this browser.
  5. Keyboard: A–E choose an answer, 1 2 3 rate your confidence, Enter checks it.
