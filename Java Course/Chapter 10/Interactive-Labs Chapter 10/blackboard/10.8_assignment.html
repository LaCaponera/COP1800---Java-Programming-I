Lab 10.8: The climbing class that said “Class full” to nobody

Your own Exception class, thrown and caught

Estimated time: about 30 minutes. Reading: Ch. 10, section 10.8 (pp. 419–421).

You will be able to:

  • Create an Exception subclass whose constructor passes a message to super()
  • Throw your exception from a method and declare it in that method's header
  • Catch your exception in the caller and respond to it with getMessage()

How to complete it

  1. Open the lab page (10.8.html) from this module. Your work saves automatically in your browser.
  2. Answer the short warm-up about the code you are given, then write your code until every structure check passes.
  3. Run the test program (instructions are on the lab page), paste its output back into the page and compare.
  4. Answer the reflection questions in your own words.
  5. Download (or copy) your lab report.

Submit here: the lab report (.txt) and your code files (ClassFullException.java, ClimbingClass.java, FrontDesk.java).

How it will be assessed

  • Requirements met (5 pts): All structure checks pass and the test program's output matches (see the lab report).
  • Reasoning (3 pts): Warm-up and reflection answers are specific to this code, in the student's own words.
  • Style (2 pts): Allman braces kept; the exception class is small and named ...Exception; the success message prints only after a successful enroll().

The checks on the lab page give you feedback; they are not your grade.
