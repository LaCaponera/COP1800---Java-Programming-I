Lab 9.6: The gift card that holds $505.0 on a $500.0 limit

Keep fields private, and let subclasses go through the parent's methods

Estimated time: about 20 minutes. Reading: Ch. 9, section 9.6 (pp. 346–347).

You will be able to:

  • Explain why information hiding keeps a class's rules in force
  • Decide between private and protected for a field a subclass wants to change
  • Change inherited data through the parent's public methods

How to complete it

  1. Open the lab page (9.6.html) from this module. Your work saves automatically in your browser.
  2. Answer the short warm-up about the code you are given, then write your code until every structure check passes.
  3. Run the test program (instructions are on the lab page), paste its output back into the page and compare.
  4. Answer the reflection questions in your own words.
  5. Download (or copy) your lab report.

Submit here: the lab report (.txt) and your code files (GiftCard.java, PromoGiftCard.java).

How it will be assessed

  • Requirements met (5 pts): All structure checks pass and the test program's output matches (see the lab report).
  • Reasoning (3 pts): Warm-up and reflection answers are specific to this code, in the student's own words.
  • Style (2 pts): Allman braces and indentation kept; every change to the balance goes through load() or spend().

The checks on the lab page give you feedback; they are not your grade.
